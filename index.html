Experiment brief
Objective
Improve purchase conversion with a personalized onboarding-to-purchase experience.
Problem
People arrive at very different stages, from an idea to a registered business. One default recommendation for everyone can lead to fewer purchases or the wrong plan.
Hypothesis
Personalizing the plan recommendation and its supporting messaging based on users' business stage and stated needs will increase purchase conversion compared with a generic recommendation.
Control (A)
Everyone sees the same plan marked Recommended.
Treatment (B)
The recommendation adapts to the answers: the plan, a reason that quotes what the user said, highlighted features, and a reminder of the answers with a way to change them.
Primary metric
Plan-to-purchase conversion rate = unique users completing a purchase ÷ unique users exposed to the plan screen. This isolates the plan step, where the treatment is.
Guardrails
Refund rate in the first 14 days, early cancellations, and average revenue per purchaser.
Decision rule
Ship B if it produces a statistically significant improvement in purchase conversion, with no material deterioration in refund rate, early cancellation rate, or revenue per purchaser. Otherwise keep A.
Before launch
Set the required sample size and test duration from the current conversion rate and the smallest improvement worth detecting.
Events to track
onboarding_started, stage_selected, logo_selected, plan_viewed (variant, recommended plan), plan_selected (matches recommendation?), checkout_started, purchase_completed (variant, plan, billing cycle, amount).
Status
Not run. This shows how I would validate the hypothesis, not a result.
